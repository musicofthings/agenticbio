# The Agentic Bio Fellowship

Do not call this a workshop or a course. The offer is **The Agentic Bio Fellowship**: a 6–8 week cohort that deploys privacy-first, local multi-agent systems for life-science workflows.

## Positioning

Senior buyers (principal investigators, clinical directors, pharma L&D, bioinformatics leads) purchase workflow transformation, speed, and data-governance — not “AI training.” The curriculum is production-grade orchestration (Docker, Nextflow DSL2, Ollama, agent loops). It is not prompt engineering.

## Pricing (INR)

| Track | Price | Who | What they get |
|---|---|---|---|
| Individual Innovator | ₹1,50,000 | Bioinformatics leads, computational biologists, clinical managers on a PD stipend | Live cohort, lab GitHub repos, Docker/Nextflow baselines, session recordings |
| Enterprise Team License | ₹5,00,000 | CRO, hospital network, biotech startup | 3–5 seats plus a private 60-minute architecture review of their stack |

₹1,000 USD-equivalent “workshop” pricing is too cheap for enterprise L&D and too expensive for casual juniors. The two-tier structure matches how those buyers actually get invoices approved: one departmental line vs many personal reimbursements.

At webinar close, give the [Manager Justification](JUSTIFICATION.md) one-pager so a director can send it to finance the same day.

## Backend funnel (after the Fellowship)

Once alumni see the cost of owning the stack, sell implementation — not more content.

1. **Fractional AI Architect** — bi-weekly review with their engineering leads (containers, Nextflow, agent-output audit). ₹2–4 lakh / month, 6-month minimum.
2. **Done-for-you deployment** — custom local or private-cloud agentic environment (e.g. VariantGPT-class or OpenClaw-class architecture on their servers). ₹25–50 lakh+ per project plus annual maintenance.
3. **Agentic Bio Consortium** — application-only alumni membership: model briefings, prompt libraries for ethics materials, PI/founder network. ₹3–5 lakh / year / seat.
